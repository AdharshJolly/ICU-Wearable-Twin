# Surviving Sepsis Campaign Guidelines 2021

**Source:** Society of Critical Care Medicine (SCCM) and European Society of Intensive Care Medicine (ESICM)
**Direct Link:** [https://www.sccm.org/SurvivingSepsisCampaign/Guidelines/Adult-Patients](https://www.sccm.org/SurvivingSepsisCampaign/Guidelines/Adult-Patients)

## The Hour-1 Bundle (Sepsis and Septic Shock)
The Hour-1 Bundle should be initiated immediately for patients presenting with sepsis or septic shock.

1. **Measure Lactate Level:** Measure lactate. Remeasure lactate if initial lactate is elevated (> 2 mmol/L).
2. **Obtain Blood Cultures:** Obtain blood cultures before administering antibiotics.
3. **Administer Broad-Spectrum Antibiotics:** Begin administration of broad-spectrum antibiotics for bacterial coverage.
4. **Administer IV Fluids:** Begin rapid administration of 30 mL/kg crystalloid for hypotension or lactate >= 4 mmol/L. This fluid resuscitation should begin immediately and be completed within 3 hours.
5. **Apply Vasopressors:** Apply vasopressors if hypotensive during or after fluid resuscitation to maintain a mean arterial pressure (MAP) >= 65 mm Hg. Norepinephrine is the first-line vasopressor.

## Hemodynamic Management
- For adults with sepsis or septic shock, we suggest using dynamic parameters (e.g., passive leg raise, stroke volume variation) over static parameters to predict fluid responsiveness.
- We recommend targeting a Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP) of 65 mm Hg for patients with septic shock requiring vasopressors.
