# American Heart Association (AHA) ACLS Guidelines 2020

**Source:** Official AHA Guidelines for CPR and Emergency Cardiovascular Care (ECC)
**Direct Link:** [https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines](https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines)

## Adult Bradycardia Algorithm
*Clinical Condition:* Heart rate typically < 50 / min.
1. Assess appropriateness for clinical condition.
2. Identify and treat underlying cause: Maintain patent airway, assist breathing as necessary. Give Oxygen if hypoxemic. Monitor ECG to identify rhythm, monitor blood pressure, and oximetry.
3. Check if Bradycardia is causing: Hypotension? Acutely altered mental status? Signs of shock? Ischemic chest discomfort? Acute heart failure?
4. IF YES (Symptomatic Bradycardia): 
   - Administer Atropine: First dose is 1 mg IV. Repeat every 3-5 minutes. Maximum: 3 mg.
   - If Atropine is ineffective: Initiate Transcutaneous Pacing OR administer Dopamine infusion (5 to 20 mcg/kg per minute) OR Epinephrine infusion (2 to 10 mcg per minute).
5. IF NO: Monitor and observe.

## Adult Tachycardia Algorithm (With Pulse)
*Clinical Condition:* Heart rate typically ≥ 150 / min.
1. Assess appropriateness for clinical condition. Heart rate typically > 150/min if tachyarrhythmia.
2. Identify and treat underlying cause: Maintain patent airway, assist breathing as necessary, give oxygen if hypoxemic, monitor ECG, BP, and oximetry.
3. Check for symptoms (Hypotension, altered mental status, shock, chest discomfort, acute heart failure).
4. IF YES (Symptomatic): Synchronized cardioversion.
5. IF NO (Stable): Ensure wide QRS complex >= 0.12 second. 
   - If wide QRS: Consider Adenosine only if regular and monomorphic. Consider antiarrhythmic infusion (Amiodarone 150 mg over 10 minutes).
   - If narrow QRS: Vagal maneuvers. Adenosine 6 mg rapid IV push. Beta blocker or calcium channel blocker.
